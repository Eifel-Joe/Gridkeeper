## How it is made

Gridkeeper is vibe-coded: its code is written by an AI, Claude, under the lead of Eifel-Joe, who designs the features,
tests them in Fusion and prints the results. Every change is checked by automated tests and real prints. Still,
expect rough edges, and please
[report what you find](https://github.com/Eifel-Joe/FusionGridfinityGenerator/issues).
