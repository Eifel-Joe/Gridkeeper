# Click test round 50: raised dividers beside a scoop wall

Add-in loaded from `wt-r50` (branch `r50-dividers-lip`), a new document. Each step: what to do, what must show.
Before and after pictures of the reported bin from the same views are kept outside the repo with the round's notes.

## The reported bin

1. Bin dialog with its defaults (2x3x5, hollow, 1.2 mm wall, lip on), **Lip type** Full, **Trim lip top** on.
   Compartments X 2, Y 3, **Dividers up to the top on grid lines** on, **Edit compartment layout** on, every
   compartment **Own** scoops with **Front** only, bin scoops off, no label tab. OK.
2. Look at the inside of a long wall where the row dividers meet it, from above. Every divider runs up to the top of
   the lip at its full thickness, its top is a flat strip as wide as the divider below; the corners left and right
   of it are rounded from the compartment up to the top, the lip's profile runs on beside it without steps, blocks
   or gaps.
3. Same at the middle column divider meeting the back wall.
4. The front row of compartments is a little shorter than the other two (about 1.4 mm), the front wall's scoop runs
   up into the lip without a step as before.
5. Inspect › Measure from the long wall's outside to a row divider's face: the dividers sit where they sit in the
   same bin without scoops (untick every compartment's front scoop and compare, then tick them again).
6. Stack a second bin of the same size on top (or place one in the assembly): its feet sit over the raised dividers,
   nothing collides.

## Other combinations

7. Same bin with bin scoop **Front** instead of the compartment scoops: the same as steps 2 to 4.
8. Lip type **Light**, compartment scoops **Right** only: the dividers meet the right wall at full thickness, the
   right column is a little narrower (about 0.7 mm).
9. **Dividers up to the top** off, same scoops: the bin builds as before round 50, the compartments are evenly
   spaced again (no shorter front row).
10. Bin with dividers up to the top and no scoops: unchanged from before.
