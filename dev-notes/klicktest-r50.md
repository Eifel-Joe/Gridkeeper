# Click test round 50: raised dividers with the lip's profile, fixes around scoops

Add-in loaded from `wt-r50` (branch `r50-dividers-lip`), a new document. Each step: what to do, what must show.
Before and after pictures (the reported bin from the same views, a section through two stacked bins, the round 48
cases with two scoop walls, four views of every divider/wall junction) are kept outside the repo with the round's
notes.

## The reported bin

1. Bin dialog with its defaults (2x3x5, hollow, 1.2 mm wall, lip on), **Lip type** Full, **Trim lip top** on.
   Compartments X 2, Y 3, **Dividers up to the top on grid lines** on, **Edit compartment layout** on, every
   compartment **Own** scoops with **Front** only, bin scoops off, no label tab. OK.
2. Look at the inside of a long wall where the row dividers meet it, from above. Every divider rises to the lip's top
   as a ridge with the lip's profile on both sides (45 degrees, straight, 45 degrees, a flat crest); where it meets the
   lip at the wall the two run into each other in a rounded corner. No steps, blocks, narrow ridges or gaps.
3. Under each ridge a support runs at 45 degrees down into the divider on both sides; the compartment corners below
   are a little tighter than before, no flat spot where corner and support meet.
4. The front row of compartments is a little shorter than the other two (about 1.4 mm). In the compartments behind a
   ridge with a front scoop, the scoop runs up into the ridge's tip without a step.
5. Place a second bin of the same size on top (or in the assembly, moved up by 35 mm): its feet sit over the ridges,
   Inspect › Interference finds none.

## No gap between a divider and its wall

At every place where a divider meets an outer wall (in the reported bin: the middle divider at the front scoop wall
and at the back wall, both row dividers at the left and right walls) look from four directions: along the wall from
one side, with the camera turned by 90 degrees (along the divider from inside the compartment), from straight above,
and from below inside the compartment up at the junction. The ridge runs into the lip without a slot or a notch at
any height, right up to the top of the lip.

6. The reported bin, at each junction as above.
7. The same bin with each lip variant: Full untrimmed, Light trimmed, Light untrimmed, Full with lip notches. At each
   junction: no gap; the outer faces and the top edge of the wall look as on a bin without dividers to the top,
   nothing sticks out of the wall or over its top.
8. Lip off: **Dividers up to the top** is greyed out, the dividers end below the top as before.

## Other combinations

9. Same bin with the bin scoop **Front** instead of the compartment scoops: as steps 2 to 4.
10. **Dividers up to the top** off, same scoops: as before round 50, compartments evenly spaced, no ridges.
11. 3x2x3, compartments X 1, Y 2, label style Cullenect, label tab on, dividers up to the top: the tab of the front
    compartment lies in front of the ridge; its label face and pocket start at the ridge's tip, the tab itself reaches
    back to the divider (no slot behind it, also with a tab top angle); an insert goes into each pocket.

## Points found on the way (p651 to p653)

12. p651: 2x3x5, Full, compartments 2x3, dividers up to the top, no scoops. Where the ridges cross: a rounded hub, no
    flat spot in the compartment corners around it.
13. p652: 2x3x1 (one height unit), bin scoop Front. The bin builds (before: an error at the floor fillet); the floor
    beside the scoop is rounded a little.
14. p653: 2x3x5, Full, compartments 1x3, dividers up to the top, bin scoops Front and Left. In the front left corner
    the cavity's corner follows the lip's inner corner up to the top, no flat crescent under the lip. The same in a bin
    without dividers to the top (for example 1x1x3 with four scoops).
